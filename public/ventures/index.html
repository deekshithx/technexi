Featured on Shark Tank, Season 3

Fractional car ownership platform, built with a founder

Owning a car outright doesn't make sense for everyone who needs one. We helped a founder build the marketplace behind it — where several owners share a single vehicle, splitting purchase cost, usage schedule, and upkeep through one platform, with none of the informal spreadsheet-and-group-chat arrangements that usually make co-ownership painful.

The platform pitched live on Shark Tank Season 3 — validating an idea that's since shaped how we approach every engagement: build for a real, specific frustration, not a market category.

Open source · Flutter package

animated_hint_textfield

A Flutter package that replaces the static placeholder text in an input field with hint text that animates in and out — the kind of small motion detail that makes a form feel considered instead of default. Drop it into any Flutter app in minutes, with no dependency baggage.

It's a small package, but it's used in production apps well beyond our own — proof that we sweat details most teams skip, all the way down to a single text field.
